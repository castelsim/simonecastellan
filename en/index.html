What I do for a concert or an event

  • Stage management and technical advance The stage agreed with the venue before load-in: scale plan, risers and equipment, schedule, crew sheets.
  • Stage plot, channel list and rider With stageplot.it: stage plot to scale, input list, monitor list and rider in PDF.
  • Live sound and recording PA, mixer, monitors and in-ears for orchestras, bands and choirs; multitrack recording, mixing and mastering, also in Dolby Atmos.
  • Production management For municipalities, local associations and companies: from the first enquiry to after the show.

Simone Castellan is a stage manager and sound engineer for concerts with orchestra and band, and the author of stageplot.it, the tool he prepares stages with. Since 2019 he has been the operations manager of a Dolby-certified Dolby Atmos 7.1.4 mixing room in Castelfranco Veneto.

He teaches Electroacoustic Music 2 at the Civica Scuola di Musica Claudio Abbado in Milan.
