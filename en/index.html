Simone Castellan is a sound engineer and electroacoustic composer. He works across the whole sound chain, from microphone to master: multitrack recording, editing, mixing and mastering for records, television and broadcast. Since 2019 he has been the operations manager of a Dolby-certified Dolby Atmos 7.1.4 mixing room in Castelfranco Veneto.

Live: technical point of contact for complete productions, multitrack concert recording, sound for events and television productions. He teaches Electroacoustic Music 2 at the Civica Scuola di Musica Claudio Abbado in Milan, and builds audio tools that run in the browser (in Italian) — acoustic measurement, system tuning, conversion — available free of charge.

The full profile lists the verifiable record credits with their catalogue codes, the electroacoustic works and the awards; the one-page CV is in Italian and downloads as a PDF.
