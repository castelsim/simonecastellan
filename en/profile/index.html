Who he is

Simone Castellan (born 1991) works across the whole sound chain, from microphone to master, as a single point of contact: multitrack recording, editing, mixing and mastering for records, television and broadcast. Since 2019 he has run a Dolby Atmos 7.1.4 Dolby-certified mixing room — twelve loudspeakers, four of them overhead. Since 2021 he has been course leader for Electroacoustic Music 2 at the Civica Scuola di Musica «Claudio Abbado», Milan's municipal music school. He has been composing electroacoustic music since 2015, with works performed in Italy, Poland, Portugal, France, Ukraine, the Czech Republic and Chile. Self-employed since 2019. Native Italian, English at B2 level.

What he does

  • Recording — microphone technique and multitrack recording in the studio and live, up to symphony orchestras of eighty players, with in-ear monitoring (each player hears their own mix on headphones).
  • Post-production — editing, mixing and mastering, immersive Dolby Atmos mixes, restoration and processing, for records, broadcast and film.
  • Live and events — PA and monitoring, sequences and playback, sound for concerts, conventions and live television.
  • Sound design and music for picture — original composition, live scoring of silent films, live electronics.
  • Tools and measurement — room acoustics and system tuning; web applications for audio production.
  • Teaching — electroacoustic music and music technology, masterclasses and workshops.

Things he has built

StagePlot — a free web application for the technical preparation of concerts and events. You draw the stage to scale by dragging microphones, instruments and lights, and the same project produces the channel list, the monitor mixes, the cabling, the power diagram and the technical rider as a PDF — the document a band sends a venue to say what it needs. Change something on stage and the documents follow. It runs in the browser, with nothing to install. It came out of a friction in the job: riders had to be redrawn by hand every time.

Free tools, all in the browser and with no account: tune a PA system by measuring it with pink noise and a microphone, generate pink noise, white noise and sweeps, work out speaker delay and delay times from BPM, find the key of a track, convert to MP3, compress images, PDFs and video. Same principle as StagePlot: when a friction keeps coming back, rather than redo the work by hand he builds the tool that removes it. (the tools are in Italian)

Il Battito di Bitcoin — an installation for «Dentro Bitcoin», the exhibition proposed by CryptoBassano for Bassano del Grappa's bid to become Italian Capital of Culture 2029. Transactions crossing the Bitcoin network at that moment become light and sound, live: every point of light is a real transfer, and a ring closes roughly every ten minutes — the average time between one block and the next. Installation and page by him, live data from mempool.space. (page in Italian)
