Who he is

Simone Castellan (born 1991) works on technology applied to sound: capture and processing chains, mixing and mastering, immersive audio, systems for concerts and events, and the software that holds a production together. He covers the whole chain, from microphone to master, as a single point of contact. Self-employed since 2019. Native Italian, English at C1 level.

Technical skills

  • Signal chain — microphone technique and multichannel recording, editing, mixing, mastering; post-production for records, broadcast and TV.
  • Immersive audio — Dolby Atmos 7.1.4 mixing in a Dolby-certified room.
  • Orchestral recording — symphony orchestras of up to 80 players, with in-ear monitoring.
  • Systems and sound reinforcement — PA, monitoring, sequences and playback for concerts, events and conventions.
  • Sound processing — restoration and processing (iZotope), sound design, music for picture.
  • Tools — Pro Tools (main), Ableton Live, Logic Pro; Yamaha DM3 console, MOTU interfaces. Mobile rig: works on site and remotely.
  • Software and automation — web applications for audio production; AI and automation applied to production workflows.

Things he has built

StagePlot — a free web application for the technical preparation of concerts and events. You draw the stage to scale by dragging microphones, instruments and lights, and the same project produces the channel list, monitor mixes, cabling, the electrical diagram and the technical rider as a PDF — all linked together. It runs in the browser, with nothing to install. It came out of a friction in the job: riders had to be redrawn by hand every time.

Free tools, all in the browser and with no account: tune a PA system by measuring it with pink noise and a microphone, generate pink noise, white noise and sweeps, work out speaker delay and delay times from BPM, find the key of a track, convert to MP3, compress images, PDFs and video. Same principle as StagePlot: when a small friction keeps coming back, he builds the tool that removes it.
