Who he is

Simone Castellan (born 1991) is a sound engineer, sound designer and electroacoustic composer, based in Cassola (Vicenza), working across northern Italy and available for projects throughout Italy and abroad. Self-employed since 2019. Native Italian, English at C1 level. Three strands to his work: sound engineer and studio manager; award-winning electroacoustic composer; lecturer in Electroacoustic Music.

Projects and vision

Beyond audio, Simone is interested in technology, AI and automation applied to music and production, and follows Bitcoin and the culture of decentralisation (as technology and culture, not as speculation). His aim is to bring music and technology together to create new ways of working and of connecting people.

His main software project is StagePlot: a free web application for live performance. You draw the stage to scale by dragging microphones, instruments and lights, and the same project produces the channel list, monitor mixes, cabling, the electrical diagram and the technical rider as a PDF — all linked together. It runs in the browser, with nothing to install. It came out of his own work: riders had to be redrawn by hand every time.

Alongside it he keeps a set of small free tools that run in the browser — pink noise, speaker delay, delay times from BPM, finding the key of a track, audio to MP3, image and PDF compression, QR codes. The principle is always the same: when a small piece of friction keeps coming back, he builds the tool that removes it instead of doing it by hand again.
